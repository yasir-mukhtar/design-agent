# UX Writing — Bahasa Indonesia (condensed from IDDS Writing Guidelines)

## 3 principles
1. **Berempati** — understand users and help them reach their goal.
2. **Berbahasa jelas dan ringkas** — simple, easy to understand.
3. **Berbasis fakta** — accurate, trustworthy.

## Sosok (voice) — define before writing
1. Understand the institution/product's role (serving citizens, providing data, setting policy).
2. Know the relationship with users (peer / operational support / authority).
3. Pick value keywords (tegas, ramah, efisien, bijak…).
4. Test with sample messages.

**Reference: Tenaga Operasional** (government default) — efisien, netral, terukur, solutif. Formal ringan, direct, accurate.
- "Data belum bisa disimpan. Periksa kembali kolom yang belum diisi."
- "Pengajuan Anda sudah diterima dan sedang diproses."

## Nada (tone) by situation
| Situation | Goal | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding / pendaftaran | welcome & motivate | ramah, suportif | "Selamat datang, mari mulai langkah pertamamu." |
| Input error / system failure | calm & help | empatik, jelas | "Data belum bisa disimpan. Periksa kembali kolom yang belum diisi." |
| Warning / batas waktu | clarity without panic | tegas, netral | "Koneksi sedang tidak stabil. Coba lagi setelah beberapa saat." |
| Official announcement | authority | formal, kredibel | "Layanan ini akan diperbarui mulai 12 Desember 2025 untuk peningkatan keamanan sistem." |
| Participation call | drive action | akrab, positif | "Yuk, bagikan pendapatmu agar layanan ini makin baik." |
| Thanks / apresiasi | show value | hangat, tulus | "Terima kasih sudah meluangkan waktu untuk melengkapi data ini." |

## Inklusivitas
- **POUR:** Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust.
- Plain language; avoid teknis/birokratis jargon; screen-reader friendly; avoid ableist assumptions ("klik saja", "sangat mudah").
- Apply across: writing (labels, errors, instructions), visual (contrast, diverse representation, legible type), interaction (accessible forms, enough touch target), basics (alt text, captions, logical nav order).

## Product microcopy — 4 core principles
1. **Fokus pada perkembangan** — show the benefit to the user's growth/progress.
2. **Tampilkan progres** — concrete progress, never "data belum lengkap".
3. **Jelaskan manfaat** — say *why* when asking for input.
4. **Berikan langkah selanjutnya** — never dead-end at "berhasil".

## Register & format rules
- Formal-but-warm. **Imperatives lead with verb + benefit:** "Tambahkan data … agar rekomendasi lebih relevan".
- "Silakan…" only for neutral instructions — not bureaucratic padding.
- **CTA = 2 words ideal, 3 words HARD MAX.** Context moves to helper text/confirmation. Long legal terms use the regulator's abbreviation ("Ajukan DKP").
- Title Case actions ("Simpan Draf", "Ajukan"), sentence case body.
- Regulator vocabulary verbatim: DKP, Deklarasi, Pengendalian, Putusan, Diperbarui…
- **Numbers in copy must be computable from the displayed data.** Targets, baselines, deadlines = assumptions → flagged in annotations + open-questions, never stated as fact.
- No em dash; short declarative sentences; numbers as people say them ("sekitar 2 poin per bulan", not "+4,3 poin/bulan").
- EYD Edisi Kelima as the spelling authority.
- Output is a **recommendation** — the instansi's UX Writer reviews.
